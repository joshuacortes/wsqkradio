ABOUT WSQK RADIO

WSQK Radio 94.5 FM was built on a simple idea: good music deserves a clear signal and a place to live. We’re an independent station focused on sound, atmosphere, and the kind of tracks that stay with you long after the dial moves on.

Our programming pulls from late-night radio culture — deep cuts, forgotten favorites, underground artists, and timeless records that don’t fit neatly into one genre. No algorithms. No playlists chasing trends. Just carefully selected music and a steady broadcast.

WSQK isn’t about hype or noise. It’s about tuning in, staying awhile, and letting the signal do its work. Whether you’re listening in the background or locked in after midnight, you’re always welcome on our frequency.
